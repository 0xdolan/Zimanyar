# Practice

`@zimanyar/practice` makes drills, schedules them and keeps the writer's mistake history. The Practice tab of `@zimanyar/app` shows it, in the extension's full view and in the desktop app.

## Drills

Every drill has a stable id, so that its schedule survives updates. `drillFor(id)` builds the drill again from the id; an id that no longer makes a drill is skipped.

| kind | id | made from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| verb | `verb:<lang>:<infinitive>:<cell>:<person>` | the first 24 reference verbs, eight tenses, generated by `@zimanyar/core` |
| noun | `noun:<lang>:<noun>:<cell>` | the vocabulary nouns: definite, indefinite, plural and ezafe forms from the declension generator |
| spot | `spot:<ruleId>:<example>` | the wrong and right sentences of each rule's examples |

A verb drill is a sentence with a gap. In the Kurmanji transitive past the agent is oblique and the verb agrees with the object (*Min tu ___*), as the course teaches. An answer is right after trimming, `normalize` and lower case; other spellings that the paradigm accepts count too. Every drill links to the lesson that teaches it.

## Spaced repetition

SuperMemo 2 (`sm2.ts`). The first try at a drill counts: right is quality 4, wrong is quality 1, which starts the card over tomorrow. Trying again after the answer is shown is practice and does not count. A card with an interval of 21 days or more is "known well".

`nextDrill` gives the drill due longest, then new spot drills for the rules among the writer's frequent mistakes, then a new drill of a random kind.

## Mistake history

The checker's `onAct` reports each finding the writer fixes (`applied`) or dismisses (`ignored`). "Fix all safe" is not recorded. `frequentMistakes` weighs a fix as 1 and a dismissal as 0.5, halving every 30 days, and leaves out notices and unknown rules. `recommendLessons` sums the weights by each rule's first lesson.

## Storage

The state is one JSON value under the key `practice`: cards, mistakes (the newest 500) and right and wrong counts for the last 90 days. `parsePractice` salvages entry by entry, so one damaged value does not lose months of reviews.

| surface | where |
| --- | --- |
| extension | `storage.local`, written by the full view and, for findings on web pages, by the background after a `record-mistake` message |
| desktop | `practice.json` beside the settings, through the `load_practice` and `save_practice` commands |
| desktop in a plain browser | `localStorage` |

Two contexts write in the extension, so every change goes through `updatePractice` (`packages/app/src/practice-store.ts`): it takes the Web Lock `zimanyar-practice`, reads the stored state, applies the change and saves. The background takes the same lock. Clearing data in settings clears practice too.

The background imports only `@zimanyar/practice/store`, which leaves the drill tables out of its bundle.

## Tests

`packages/practice/test` covers the schedule, every drill id, answers, the history and salvage. `scripts/screenshots/desktop.spec.ts` runs the drill flow end to end: a fixed finding in the checker, a wrong answer and then a right one, progress, the lesson link and a reload.
