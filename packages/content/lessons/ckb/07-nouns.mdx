## The bare noun

A noun without endings is **generic**: پیاو means *man* in general, or *a man* when the meaning is clear. Sorani has no words for *the* or *a*. Both are **endings**:

| meaning | ending after a consonant | after a vowel | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the** (singular) | **-ەکە** -eke | **-کە** -ke (**-یەکە** after ی / ێ) | پیاوەکە *the man* |
| **the** (plural) | **-ەکان** -ekan | **-کان** -kan (**-یەکان** after ی / ێ) | پیاوەکان *the men* |
| **a / one** | **-ێک** -êk | **-یەک** -yek | پیاوێک *a man* |
| plural (generic) | **-ان** -an | **-یان** -yan | پیاوان *men* |

## Consonant-final and vowel-final

The choice depends on how the noun **ends in pronunciation**, not on the last letter you see. پیاو ends in the consonant *w* (because و after ا is a consonant, lesson 3), so it takes the consonant forms; دەرگا *door* ends in the vowel *a*.

The table below is generated by zimankit's `decline()` function, which applies exactly these rules:

<NounTable lang="ckb" nouns="پیاو,ژن,کتێب,ماڵ,دەرگا,مامۆستا,ماسی,خانە,چیا" cells="bare,def.sg,def.pl,ind.sg,pl" />

Things to notice:

* ماسی ends in the vowel *î*, so a **glide** ی is written before the ending: ماسییەکە *masîyeke*, ماسییەک.
* خانە ends in ە: the definite is خانەکە, and the generic plural *-an* is not used with it (say خانەکان or چەند خانەیەک *several houses*). That cell is empty in the table.
* Generic plurals with **-ان** are common in writing (پیاوان *men*); speech prefers the definite plural (پیاوەکان).

## Counting

After a number the noun stays **singular and bare**: سێ پیاو *three men*, دوو کتێب *two books*. To say *the three men*: سێ پیاوەکە.

## How zimankit reads these forms

The analyzer splits a word into stem and endings. This is the same logic as above, run backwards:

<Analyze lang="ckb" words="پیاوەکە,ژنەکان,کتێبێک,دەرگاکە,ماسییەکە,مامۆستایان" />

Look at the last row. **مامۆستایان** can mean *teachers* (plural -یان after a vowel) or *their teacher* (the 3pl personal ending -یان, lesson 11). Out of context the analyzer prefers the second reading. Only the sentence can decide, which is why a grammar checker must look at context, not single words.

## Summary

* *the* = -ەکە / -کە, *the (pl)* = -ەکان / -کان, *a* = -ێک / -یەک, plural = -ان / -یان.
* Choose by the **sound** at the end of the noun: consonant or vowel.
* After numbers the noun stays singular.
