## The subjunctive

The **subjunctive** is the verb form for things that are wanted, possible, uncertain or not yet real: *that I go*, *(so) that I may go*, *if I go*. It looks like the present with **ب** instead of **دە**:

> **ب** + present stem + set-1 ending (م یت ێت ین ن ن); negative **نە**

## The past subjunctive

The past stem takes **با** for an unreal or wished-for past, and **بێت** for a possible one. Past-perfect **بووبا** puts it further back. A transitive verb adds the agent clitic, and an intransitive verb adds the normal past endings:

| | transitive | intransitive |
|---|---|---|
| با | خواردبام, خواردبات, خواردبای | هاتبام, هاتبایت, هاتبا |
| بێت | خواردبێتم, خواردبێتت, خواردبێتی | هاتبێتم, هاتبێتیت, هاتبێت |
| بووبا | خواردبووبام | هاتبووبام |

## When to use it

| after | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **دەمەوێت** | I want (to) | دەمەوێت بچم. *I want to go.* |
| **دەتوانم** | I can | دەتوانم بێم. *I can come.* |
| **با** | let's / let | با بڕۆین! *Let's go!* |
| **پێویستە** | it is necessary, must | پێویستە بخوێنیت. *You must study.* |
| **ئەگەر** | if | ئەگەر بێیت، دەچین. *If you come, we'll go.* |
| **بۆ ئەوەی** | in order to | هاتم بۆ ئەوەی ببینمت. *I came to see you.* |

## Practice

1. *I want to eat.*
2. *Write!* (to one person) and *Don't write!* (to several).
3. *We can't go.*

## Summary

* Subjunctive = **ب** + present stem + ending (negative **نە**), used after *want, can, must, let's, if, in order to*.
* ویستن: دەمەوێت، دەتەوێت، دەیەوێت…
* Imperative **ب…ە / ب…ن**, prohibitive **مە…**; وەرە *come!*, بچۆ or بڕۆ *go!*
