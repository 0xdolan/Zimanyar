## Comparative and superlative

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| comparative *-er* | **-تر** | گەورە*تر* *bigger* |
| superlative *-est* | **-ترین** | گەورە*ترین* *biggest* |

* *than* is **لە**: ئەو **لە** من گەورەترە. *He is bigger / older than me.*
* The superlative stands **before** the noun, without ezafe: گەورەترین شار *the biggest city*.
* The separate word **تر** means *other*: کابرایەکی تر *another man*. Do not confuse it with -تر *-er*, which is joined.

## Building words with suffixes

Sorani creates many words with a small set of suffixes. Once you know them you can guess the meaning of thousands of new words:

| suffix | makes | example | from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ی** | adjectives of origin, languages | کوردی *Kurdish* | کورد *Kurd* |
| **-ایەتی / -یەتی** | abstract nouns | مرۆڤایەتی *humanity* | مرۆڤ *human* |
| **-ستان** | places, countries | کوردستان *Kurdistan* | کورد |
| **-کار** | people who do something | کرێکار *worker* | کرێ *wage* |
| **-دار** | people/things that have something | پارەدار *rich* | پارە *money* |
| **-انە** | adverbs (*-ly*), *-wise* | ڕۆژانە *daily* | ڕۆژ *day* |
| **-ە** | nouns and past-stem adjectives | چاکە *goodness*, کردە *done* | چاک، کرد |
| **-گەر، -چی، -وان** | people who work with something | ئاسنگەر، ڕاوچی، باخەوان | ئاسن، ڕاو، باخ |
| **-زانی، -ناسی** | -logy, -nomy | زیندەزانی، ئەستێرەناسی | |
| **بێ-، نا-، هاو-** | without, not, co- | بێکەس، ناپاک، هاوکار | |

## How zimankit takes words apart

The analyzer recognises these suffixes and also tells you when it is unsure:

<Analyze lang="ckb" words="گەورەتر,گەورەترین,کوردستان,ڕۆژانە,پارەدار,کرێکار" />

Derivational suffixes are less reliable than inflectional ones. *-ی* can be a nisba, an ezafe or a clitic, and many words that look derived are simply lemmas, so the analyzer gives them a lower score unless the stem is a known word.

## Summary

* **-تر** comparative with **لە** *than*; **-ترین** superlative before the noun.
* Word-building suffixes include -ی، -ایەتی، -ستان، -کار، -دار، -انە، -گەر، -زانی.
