## This and that

Sorani frames the noun between a **demonstrative before it** and the ending **-ە** (after a vowel **-یە**) after it:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| this | **ئەم** … **ە** | **ئەم** … **انە** |
| that | **ئەو** … **ە** | **ئەو** … **انە** |

<NounTable lang="ckb" nouns="پیاو,کتێب,دەرگا,ماسی,خانە" cells="bare,dem" />

<Sentences lang="ckb" ipa items={[["ئەم کتێبە باشە.","This book is good."],["ئەو پیاوە مامۆستایە.","That man is a teacher."],["ئەم پیاوانە کوردن.","These men are Kurds."],["ئەو دەرگایە داخە.","Close that door."]]} />

Used **alone** (without a noun), *this* and *that* are pronouns:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| this (one) | **ئەمە** | **ئەمانە** |
| that (one) | **ئەوە** | **ئەوانە** |

ئەمە چییە؟ *What is this?*, ئەوە کتێبە. *That is a book.*

## Question words

| word | Latin | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **کێ** | kê | who |
| **چی** | çî | what |
| **کام** | kam | which |
| **چۆن** | çon | how |
| **کەی** | key | when |
| **بۆچی** | boçî | why |
| **لەکوێ** | lekwê | where |
| **کوا** | kwa | where is …? |
| **چەند** | çend | how many, how much |

The question word usually stands **where the answer would be**, which is often just before the verb:

<Sentences lang="ckb" items={[["ئەوە کێیە؟","Who is that?"],["ئەمە چییە؟","What is this?"],["ناوت چییە؟","What is your name?"],["لەکوێ دەژیت؟","Where do you live?"],["بۆچی هاتیت؟","Why did you come?"],["چەند کتێبت هەیە؟","How many books do you have?"]]} />

## Summary

* *this / that + noun*: ئەم / ئەو … ە (plural … انە).
* Alone: ئەمە، ئەوە، ئەمانە، ئەوانە.
* Question words go where the answer would be.
