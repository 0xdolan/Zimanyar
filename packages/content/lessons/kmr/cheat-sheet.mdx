## Pronouns and copula [6]

| person | direct | oblique | copula (after C / V) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | min | im / me |
| 2sg | tu | te | î / yî |
| 3sg | ew, ev | wî / wê, vî / vê | e / ye |
| 1pl | em | me | in / ne |
| 2pl | hûn | we | in / ne |
| 3pl | ew, ev | wan, van | in / ne |

## Noun endings [7 to 9]

| | feminine | masculine | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| direct (the) | · | · | · |
| oblique | -ê | -î (optional) | -an |
| ezafe | -a | -ê | -ên |
| indefinite direct | -ek | -ek | hin … |
| indefinite oblique | -ekê | -ekî | |
| indefinite ezafe | -eke | -ekî | |
| vocative | -ê | -o | -ino |

After a vowel: y is inserted (çiyayê, xaniyê).

**Oblique is used:** after prepositions · for present-tense objects · for owners after ezafe · for past transitive agents.

## Ergativity [14, 22]

| | agent | object | verb agrees with |
|---|---|---|---|
| present-stem tenses | direct | oblique | agent |
| past, transitive | **oblique** | **direct** | **object** |
| past, intransitive | direct | · | subject |
