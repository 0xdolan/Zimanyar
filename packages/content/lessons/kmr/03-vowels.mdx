## Eight vowels

Kurmanji has eight vowels: five **long** and three **short**. Each has its own letter:

| letter | IPA | sounds like | length | example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **a** | aː | *a* in *father* | long | av *water* |
| **ê** | eː | *ay* in *say*, without the glide | long | rê *road* |
| **î** | iː | *ee* in *see* | long | şîr *milk* |
| **o** | oː | *o* in *more* | long | roj *day* |
| **û** | uː | *oo* in *food* | long | çûn *to go* |
| **e** | ɛ | *e* in *bed* | short | dest *hand* |
| **i** | ɪ | *i* in *bit*, often weaker | short | jin *woman* |
| **u** | ʊ | *u* in *put* | short | kurd *Kurd* |

## Pairs to practise

The marked vowels are long; the unmarked *e i u* are short. Getting this right changes the word:

| short | long |
|---|---|
| **e** · *şer* /ʃɛɾ/ war | **ê** · *şêr* /ʃeːɾ/ lion |
| **i** · *dil* /dɪl/ heart | **î** · *dîl* /diːl/ captive |
| **u** · *kur* /kʊɾ/ boy, son | **û** · *kûr* /kuːɾ/ deep |

## The weak i

Short **i** is often reduced to a quick central vowel, especially in the last syllable of a word, as in the infinitive ending *-in*. zimankit writes it as [ɯ] in that position, which makes the Kurmanji transcription parallel to Sorani, where the same vowel is not even written:

<Words lang="kmr" words="dîtin,xwarin,kirin,nivîsîn" />

## xw: a rounded x

The combination **xw** is pronounced as one sound, an *x* with rounded lips: *xwe* /xʷɛ/ “self”, *xwarin* /xʷaːɾɯn/ “to eat”, *xwendin* /xʷɛndɯn/ “to read”. In many dialects the *w* colours the following vowel, so *xwarin* can sound like *xorin*.

## Vowels next to vowels

Two vowels rarely touch in Kurmanji. When an ending starting with a vowel is added to a word ending in a vowel, a **y** is inserted, and a final **î** becomes short **i** before it:

| word | + -ek *a* | + -ê (ezafe) |
|---|---|---|
| çiya *mountain* | çiyayek | çiyayê |
| xanî *house* | xaniyek | xaniyê |
| mamoste *teacher* | mamosteyek | mamosteyê |

You will meet these endings in lessons 7 to 9; zimankit applies the rule automatically.

## Practice

1. Which vowels are long: a, e, ê, i, î, o, u, û?
2. Pronounce *kur* and *kûr*. What do they mean?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. a, ê, î, o, û.
2. /kʊɾ/ *boy, son*, /kuːɾ/ *deep*.

</details>

## Summary

* Long: **a ê î o û**; short: **e i u**.
* Short *i* is often reduced, especially in *-in*.
* **xw** is one rounded sound.
* Between two vowels a **y** appears: çiyayek, xaniyê.
