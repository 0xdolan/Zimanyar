## Two sets of pronouns

Kurmanji pronouns have two forms. The **direct** form is used for the subject of most sentences. The **oblique** form is used after prepositions, for objects of present-tense verbs, for owners (*my, your*) and for the doer in the past tense (lesson 14).

| person | direct | oblique | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **ez** | **min** | I / me, my |
| 2sg | **tu** | **te** | you / you, your |
| 3sg (that) | **ew** | **wî** (m) / **wê** (f) | he, she, it / him, her, his, her |
| 3sg (this) | **ev** | **vî** (m) / **vê** (f) | this one |
| 1pl | **em** | **me** | we / us, our |
| 2pl | **hûn** | **we** | you (pl) / you, your |
| 3pl (those) | **ew** | **wan** | they / them, their |
| 3pl (these) | **ev** | **van** | these |

Gender appears only in the oblique 3sg: *wî* for a man (or masculine noun), *wê* for a woman (or feminine noun).

## “To be”: the copula

*Am, are, is* is a short **word written after** the noun or adjective:

| person | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **im** | **me** |
| 2sg | **î** | **yî** |
| 3sg | **e** | **ye** |
| 1pl / 2pl / 3pl | **in** | **ne** |

<Sentences lang="kmr" ipa items={[["Ez kurd im.","I am Kurdish."],["Tu kurd î.","You are Kurdish."],["Ew kurd e.","He / She is Kurdish."],["Em kurd in.","We are Kurdish."],["Ez mamoste me.","I am a teacher."],["Tu mamoste yî.","You are a teacher."],["Ew mamoste ye.","He / She is a teacher."],["Hûn mamoste ne.","You (pl) are teachers."]]} />

The plural has one form for all persons: *em / hûn / ew … in*.

## “Not to be”

Put **ne** before the noun or adjective; the copula stays at the end:

<Sentences lang="kmr" items={[["Ez ne mamoste me.","I am not a teacher."],["Ew ne li malê ye.","He is not at home."]]} />

## “There is” and “to have”: heye / tune

**heye** *there is* (plural **hene**), negative **tune** or **tuneye**. With an owner in the oblique (or an ezafe phrase) it means *to have*:

<Sentences lang="kmr" ipa items={[["Av heye.","There is water."],["Nan tune.","There is no bread."],["Pirtûkeke min heye.","I have a book."],["Du birayên min hene.","I have two brothers."]]} />

## Questions

A yes/no question can be a statement with a rising tone, or start with the particle **ma**: *Ma tu kurd î?* “Are you Kurdish?”

## Practice

1. *I am a student* (*xwendekar*).
2. *We are not teachers.*
3. *She is beautiful* (*ciwan*).

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Ez xwendekar im.
2. Em ne mamoste ne.
3. Ew ciwan e.

</details>

## Summary

* Direct: ez tu ew em hûn ew; oblique: min te wî/wê me we wan.
* Copula after the word: im î e in (after vowels me yî ye ne).
* *Not*: ne … ; *there is*: heye/hene, negative tune.
