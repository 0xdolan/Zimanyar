## Consonants like English

**b d f g h k l m n p s t v w y z** are pronounced much as in English. **g** is always hard (*go*), **h** is always pronounced, also at the end of a word (*deh* “ten”), **s** is always *s* (never *z*).

## Consonants with a special letter

| letter | IPA | as in | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **c** | dʒ | *jam* | ciwan *young* |
| **ç** | tʃ | *church* | çav *eye* |
| **j** | ʒ | *measure* | jin *woman* |
| **ş** | ʃ | *ship* | şev *night* |
| **x** | x | Scottish *loch* | xew *sleep* |
| **q** | q | a *k* deep in the throat | qelem *pen* |

## r and rr

Kurmanji has two r-sounds:

* a **tap** (one flick of the tongue, like American *butter*), written **r** between vowels and at the end: *bira* /bɪɾaː/ “brother”;
* a **trill** (rolled), written **rr** inside a word and **r** at the start of a word, where only the trill occurs: *roj* /roːʒ/ “day”, *kerr* /kɛr/ “deaf”.

The difference changes meaning: **ker** *donkey*, **kerr** *deaf*.

## Aspirated and unaspirated p, t, k, ç

Kurmanji distinguishes a **breathy** (aspirated) and a **tight** (unaspirated) version of p, t, k and ç. The breathy one is like English *p* in *pin*; the tight one is like the *p* in *spin*. The Hawar alphabet does **not** write the difference, so you learn it word by word from listening. Dictionaries sometimes mark the tight one with an apostrophe (*p'*). zimankit's narrow transcription mode shows both: plain letters as [pʰ tʰ kʰ tʃʰ], the apostrophe forms without aspiration.

## Stress

Most words are stressed on their **last syllable**: *zi-MAN* “language”, *ki-TÊB* “book”, *ba-JAR* “city”. Case and ezafe endings, which you will meet soon, are usually unstressed, and the negative prefixes *na-* and *ne-* of verbs carry the stress.

## Practice

1. Is the r in *roj* a tap or a trill? And in *bira*?
2. What is the difference between *ker* and *kerr*?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. *roj*: trill (word-initial). *bira*: tap.
2. *ker* donkey (tap), *kerr* deaf (trill).

</details>

## Summary

* c = j(am), ç = ch, j = (mea)s(ure), ş = sh, x = (lo)ch, q = deep k.
* r = tap, rr and initial r = trill.
* p t k ç come in aspirated and unaspirated variants that the spelling does not show.
