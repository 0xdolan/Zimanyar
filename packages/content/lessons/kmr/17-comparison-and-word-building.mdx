## Comparative and superlative

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| comparative *-er* | **-tir** | mezin**tir** *bigger* |
| superlative *-est* | **-tirîn** | mezin**tirîn** *biggest* |

* *than* is **ji**: *Ew **ji** min mezintir e.* “He is older (bigger) than me.”
* The superlative stands **before** the noun without ezafe: *mezintirîn bajar* “the biggest city”. Also common: **herî** + adjective after the noun: *bajarê herî mezin*.

## Building words with suffixes

| suffix | makes | example | from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-î** | adjectives of origin, languages; abstract nouns | kurdî *Kurdish* | kurd |
| **-tî / -ahî** | abstract nouns | hevaltî *friendship*, bilindahî *height* | heval *friend*, bilind *high* |
| **-van / -kar / -ger** | people who do something | rojnamevan *journalist*, xebatkar *worker* | rojname *newspaper*, xebat *work* |
| **-dar** | having | birîndar *wounded* | birîn *wound* |
| **-stan / -geh / -xane** | places | Kurdistan, zanîngeh *university*, nexweşxane *hospital* | zanîn *knowing*, nexweş *sick* |
| **-ane** | adverbs, *-ly* | mêrane *bravely* | mêr *man* |
| **-ik / -ok** | diminutives | keçik *little girl* | keç *girl* |

## How zimankit takes words apart

<Analyze lang="kmr" words="mezintir,mezintirîn,Kurdistan,hevaltî,rojnamevan,birîndar,zanîngeh" />

As in Sorani, derivational suffixes are less reliable than inflections: *-î* can be a derivation, the masculine oblique or the copula *î*. The analyzer gives a derived reading high confidence only when the stem is a known word.

## Practice

1. *more beautiful* (*xweşik*).
2. *the biggest house* (*xanî*).
3. What does **xwendekar** mean? (*xwendin* to study)

## Summary

* **-tir** + **ji** *than*; **-tirîn** before the noun, or **herî** after it.
* Suffixes: -î, -tî/-ahî, -van/-kar/-ger, -dar, -stan/-geh/-xane, -ane, -ik.
