## Simple prepositions

A noun or pronoun after a preposition is always **oblique**.

| preposition | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **li** | at, in, on | li malê *at home* |
| **ji** | from, of | ji Amedê *from Amed* |
| **bi** | with, by (means) | bi trênê *by train* |
| **bo / ji bo** | for | ji bo te *for you* |
| **heta** | until, as far as | heta sibê *until tomorrow* |
| **bê** | without | bê pere *without money* |
| **wek / mîna** | like | wek bavê xwe *like his father* |

Place words combine with *li* and *ji*: **li ser** *on*, **li bin** *under*, **li ber** *in front of*, **li pişt** *behind*, **li nav** *inside*, **li kêleka** *beside*.

## Circumpositions

Kurmanji often frames the noun with a preposition **and** a postposition:

| frame | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **di … de** | in, inside | di malê de *in the house* |
| **ji … re** | to, for (a person) | ji min re *to/for me* |
| **bi … re** | with (together) | bi min re *with me* |
| **ji … ve** | since, from | ji sibê ve *since the morning* |
| **di … re** | through, via | di Stenbolê re *via Istanbul* |

## Contracted forms

When a 3sg pronoun (*wî / wê*) follows **ji, bi, li, di**, the two fuse into one word:

| | + wî / wê | example |
|---|---|---|
| ji | **jê** | jê re *to him/her* |
| bi | **pê** | pê re *with him/her* |
| li | **lê** | lê dinêre *looks at him/her* |
| di | **tê** | tê de *in it* |

## Summary

* After a preposition the noun is **oblique**.
* Circumpositions: di…de *in*, ji…re *for/to*, bi…re *with*, ji…ve *since*.
* ji/bi/li/di + 3sg pronoun → jê, pê, lê, tê.
