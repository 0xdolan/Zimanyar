Kurmanji endings are regular. What you have to memorise is the **present stem** of each verb and a few contractions.

## hatin *to come*

Present stem **ê**, which contracts with the prefixes: **têm** (di + ê), **nayêm**, **bêm**, **neyêm**; imperative **were / werin**, prohibitive **neyê / neyên**.

## anîn *to bring*

Present stem **în**: **tînim** *I bring*, **nayînim**, **bînim**.

<VerbTable lang="kmr" verb="anîn" cells="present,present_neg,subj,past" />

## çûn *to go*

Regular present (*diçim*), but the imperative is **here / herin** *go!*

<VerbTable lang="kmr" verb="çûn" cells="present,past,imperative" />

## zanîn *to know* and karîn *can*

Negated with **ni-** instead of *na-*: **nizanim** *I don't know*, **nikarim** *I can't*.

<VerbTable lang="kmr" verb="zanîn" cells="present,present_neg,past" />

## bûn *to be, to become*

*bûn* is *to become* and supplies the subjunctive and past of *to be*: **dibim** *I become*, **bibim** *(that) I be*, **bûm** *I was / became*. The present *am / is / are* is the copula of lesson 6.

<VerbTable lang="kmr" verb="bûn" cells="present,subj,past,perfect" />

*To have* in the past and subjunctive uses **hebûn**: *pirtûkeke min hebû* “I had a book”.

## Unpredictable present stems

These are the stems to memorise. The endings are regular:

| infinitive | present stem | 1sg present |
|---|---|---|
| kirin *do* | k | dikim |
| xwarin *eat* | xw | dixwim |
| dîtin *see* | bîn | dibînim |
| gotin *say* | bêj | dibêjim |
| dan *give* | d | didim |
| birin *carry* | b | dibim |
| xwestin *want* | xwaz | dixwazim |
| man *stay* | mîn | dimînim |
| kuştin *kill* | kuj | dikujim |
| şûştin *wash* | şo | dişom |
| hiştin *leave* | hêl | dihêlim |
| bihîstin *hear* | bihîz | dibihîzim |
| firotin *sell* | firoş | difiroşim |

Notice that *dibim* is both *I become* (bûn) and *I carry* (birin). Context decides; the analyzer returns both readings.

## Summary

* hatin: têm / nayêm / bêm / were; anîn: tînim.
* çûn: here!, zanîn, karîn: ni-.
* bûn: become / was; hebûn: had.
* Learn the present stem of every verb.
