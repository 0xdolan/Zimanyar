## Demonstratives

*This* is **ev**, *that* is **ew**. Like pronouns, they have direct and oblique forms, and in the oblique the noun takes its oblique ending too:

| | direct | oblique feminine | oblique masculine | oblique plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| this | **ev** jin | **vê** jinê | **vî** mirovî | **van** jinan |
| that | **ew** jin | **wê** jinê | **wî** mirovî | **wan** jinan |

<Sentences lang="kmr" ipa items={[["Ev pirtûk nû ye.","This book is new."],["Ew mirov mamoste ye.","That man is a teacher."],["Ez vê pirtûkê dixwînim.","I am reading this book."],["Ez wî mirovî nas dikim.","I know that man."],["Van rojan pir germ e.","These days it is very hot."]]} />

The masculine oblique ending **-î**, often dropped elsewhere, is normal after *vî / wî*: *vî bajarî* “this city”.

Alone, **ev** and **ew** mean *this one*, *that one*, and also *he / she / it / they*: *Ev çi ye?* “What is this?”

## Question words

| word | meaning |
|---|---|
| **kî** | who |
| **çi** | what |
| **kîjan** | which |
| **çawa** | how |
| **çima** | why |
| **kengî** | when |
| **li ku** / **ku derê** | where |
| **ji ku** | from where |
| **çend** | how many, how much |
| **ma** | yes/no question particle |

The question word stands where the answer would be, usually before the verb:

<Sentences lang="kmr" items={[["Ev kî ye?","Who is this?"],["Navê te çi ye?","What is your name?"],["Tu li ku dijî?","Where do you live?"],["Tu ji ku yî?","Where are you from?"],["Çima tu nayêyî?","Why don't you come?"],["Çend pirtûkên te hene?","How many books do you have?"]]} />

## Practice

1. *this house* (*mal f*), direct and oblique.
2. *I see those men* (*mirov*, present *dibînim*).

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. ev mal, vê malê
2. Ez wan mirovan dibînim.

</details>

## Summary

* ev / ew (direct); vê, vî, van / wê, wî, wan (oblique) + oblique ending on the noun.
* Question words: kî çi kîjan çawa çima kengî li ku çend; particle ma.
