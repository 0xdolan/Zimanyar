title: Adding a rule
description: What a checking rule consists of.
sidebar:

---

A rule lives in `packages/rules/src/<lang>/<family>/` as three files:

- `<rule>.ts`, the check itself
- `<rule>.meta.yaml`, with its severity, messages in English, Sorani and Kurmanji, reason, examples and lesson links
- `<rule>.test.ts`, with at least ten sentences it must flag and ten it must not

Then run `make rules` to compile the metadata, and `make corpus` to measure the rule on the gold corpus. The rule's page on this site is generated from its metadata.
