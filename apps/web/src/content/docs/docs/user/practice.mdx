The Practice tab is in the extension's full view and in the desktop app. Everything it keeps stays on your device.

## Drills

Choose Sorani or Kurmanji and the kinds of drill:

- **Verb forms**: type the verb that fills the gap, in the tense shown.
- **Nouns and ezafe**: type the form of the noun that is asked for.
- **Spot the mistake**: choose the correct sentence of two.

Press Enter to check. If the answer is wrong, it is shown, and you can try again. Every drill links to the lesson that teaches it.

## Reviews

A drill you answer right comes back after a day, then six days, then longer each time. A drill you get wrong comes back the next day. Only your first try counts. **Due now** shows how many drills are waiting; the Practice tab shows the same number when *Show how many drills are due* is on in settings.

## Your frequent mistakes

Findings you fix or dismiss in the checker are remembered, and the rules behind them are listed with the lessons to read. Recent mistakes count more than old ones. Clearing your data in settings clears this history and your reviews.
