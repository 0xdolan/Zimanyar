## A correct word is underlined

The lexicon does not hold every word, especially names and new words. Choose **Ignore** on the card. If a rule is wrong about a sentence, please [open an issue](https://github.com/0xdolan/Zimanyar/issues) with the sentence.

## There are no spelling suggestions at first

The checker loads about 2 MB of word data for each variety when it first needs it. Grammar works straight away, and spelling joins in once that has loaded.

## It is checking in the wrong variety

Very short texts are hard to identify. Choose the variety above the text field.

## It stopped working offline

The site is kept for offline use after your first visit. Clearing your browser's site data removes that copy, and the next visit online restores it.
