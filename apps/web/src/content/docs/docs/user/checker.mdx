The [checker](../../../check/) runs in your browser. Your text stays on your device, and it keeps working offline after your first visit.

## Checking

Type or paste into the text field. Underlines appear as you write, and their shape says what they mean:

| underline | meaning |
|---|---|
| red and wavy | an error |
| brown and dotted | a warning |
| blue and dashed | a hint |
| red and straight | a word it does not know |

The language is detected for each paragraph. Choose **Sorani** or **Kurmanji** to fix it to one.

## Fixing

Select an underlined word or a line in the list of findings to see what is wrong, why, and how to fix it. Press a suggestion to apply it, and undo with Ctrl+Z as with any typing.

**Fix all safe** applies only the fixes that cannot change meaning, such as wrong letter codes. Grammar is always left for you to decide.

## Sharing

**Share** copies a link that holds your text after the `#`. Browsers never send that part to a server, so the text reaches only the people you give the link to.

## Strictness

Rules follow the written standard. Some colloquial forms that are correct in speech are flagged for that reason, and a setting for accepting them comes with the settings page.
